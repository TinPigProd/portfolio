About TinPigProductions

A learning design
practice built for
organizations that have
outgrown their systems.

TinPigProductions is the independent practice behind the FictionalPig engagement a consultancy that builds AI-enabled talent and learning infrastructure for high-growth organizations at the point where their existing systems have stopped keeping pace.

The work sits at the intersection of people strategy, instructional design, and AI enablement not as adjacent disciplines but as a single interconnected system. Competency frameworks that connect to feedback tools. Feedback tools that trigger learning. Learning that surfaces in the workflow rather than adjacent to it.

The practice draws on 10 years of building capability systems across enterprise, high-growth, and SaaS environments including enterprise leadership frameworks at a global manufacturing organization, LMS and LXP ecosystem architecture for customer and partner audiences at a leading storage technology company, and core talent infrastructure design at a rapidly scaling technology organization.

TinPigProductions is Prosci-certified in change management because the best-designed system still fails if the people inside the organization aren't brought along with it. Change enablement is not an add-on. It is part of the architecture.

How TinPigProductions works

Systems thinking.
Iterative delivery. Human center.

01
Understand before designing
Every engagement starts with the root cause, not the solution. The right learning strategy is the one that addresses the actual gap not the gap that's easiest to train around. I cringe over training for training's sake.
02
Move quickly from ambiguity
Iterative design approaches from agile cycles move fast from ambiguous problem to structured direction without overbuilding upfront. Rapid prototyping surfaces what's working before full-scale commitment.
03
AI as infrastructure, not novelty
Claude, Copilot, and Runway are embedded throughout the process as practical tools for synthesis, structuring complex inputs, and accelerating how expert knowledge becomes something others can use.
04
Design for the real workflow
In-platform over adjacent. Learning that requires leaving the flow of work to go find it is learning that doesn't happen. Every design decision starts with where people actually are, not where they should be.
05
Short feedback loops
What is actually landing in practice matters more than what was designed to land. Incremental refinement through use, not large infrequent releases that have already aged out of relevance.
06
Change built in, not bolted on
Prosci-certified change management is part of every engagement because even the best-designed system fails if the people inside the organization aren't supported through the transition.
