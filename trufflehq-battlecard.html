The 30-second pitch
Memorize the idea, not the words.

Most organizations know they have a skills gap. They don't know where it is, how big it is, or which gaps are actually costing them. TruffleHQ's Workforce Intelligence Platform surfaces that picture automatically, connecting the skills your people have today to the roles and projects you need to fill tomorrow. Our AI-powered skills gap analysis does it in real time, so HR isn't waiting for the annual engagement survey to find out what they're missing.

Before the deal is real
M
Metrics
What does success look like in numbers?
▼
If TruffleHQ solved this in 12 months, how would you measure that? What number moves?
Push for something specific. Unfilled roles, external hire costs, time-to-fill, AI readiness score. A prospect who can't name a metric doesn't have active pain yet.
⚠ No metric = no urgency. Find it or disqualify.
E
Economic Buyer
Who controls the budget and signs the contract?
▼
Who would need to approve an investment at this level? Have you bought similar platforms before, and who was involved?
Rarely the person you're talking to. Get to the economic buyer before final stages or the deal stalls at approval.
⚠ If you can't name them, you don't have access yet.
D
Decision Criteria
What does the evaluation actually look for?
▼
When you evaluate options, what are the two or three things that matter most? Not what the vendor says. What actually matters to you.
Their answer tells you what to prove and what to deprioritize. HCM integration, time to insight, adoption ease. Know which one is the real gate.
D
Decision Process
How do decisions like this actually get made?
▼
Walk me through how a decision like this moves forward. Who gets involved, in what order, and what does final approval look like?
Surface procurement and legal early. Both will appear as surprise blockers if you don't. Get them moving in parallel, not after a verbal yes.
⚠ "We'd need to involve IT" is not a no. Start that process now.
I
Identify Pain
Who feels it and what does it cost them?
▼
What decision are you making right now without the data you wish you had? Has that gap cost you something concrete this year?
CHRO / CPO
Board is asking about AI readiness. Every answer they give is a guess dressed up as strategy.
L&D Director
Building programs without knowing which skills actually need development. Budget spent on what's available, not what's needed.
HR Business Partner
Talent reviews feel like politics. No shared language for what good looks like.
Workforce Planning
Hiring externally for gaps that already exist internally, just invisibly.
⚠ General awareness of a skills gap is not pain. A specific bad hire or missed internal promotion is pain.
C
Champion
Who sells TruffleHQ when you're not in the room?
▼
If I sent you a summary of what we discussed, would you be comfortable sharing it with your leadership team? Who else would benefit from seeing this?
A champion who won't advocate internally is a contact. They need access to the economic buyer, motivation to change, and credibility to influence. Confirm all three before you count on them.
⚠ Enthusiasm in the meeting ≠ advocacy after it.

Objection
"We already have a skills taxonomy in our HCM."
▼
A taxonomy tells you what skills exist on paper. TruffleHQ tells you where they actually live, how strong they are, and how they compare to what your roadmap requires. Most HCMs capture skills at hire and never update them. That is not intelligence. That is a snapshot from three years ago.
→ Decision Criteria
Objection
"Our employees won't keep skills data up to date."
▼
TruffleHQ doesn't rely on self-reported skills. The AI infers skill signals from work activity, project history, and performance data, so the picture updates continuously without requiring employees to maintain their own profiles.
→ Decision Criteria
Objection
"We don't have budget right now."
▼
What's the cost of your last external hire for a role someone internal could have filled? Most customers find one prevented mis-hire covers the platform cost for the year. The question isn't whether you can afford TruffleHQ. It is whether you can afford to keep making workforce decisions without it.
→ Metrics + Economic Buyer
Objection
"We need to involve IT and security first."
▼
Expected and appropriate. We have a standard security review package covering SOC 2 Type II, data residency, and integration architecture. Most reviews complete in two to three weeks. Let's get that started now so it runs in parallel and doesn't become the long pole.
→ Decision Process
Closing
"We just need more time to evaluate."
▼
What specifically are you evaluating that you don't have clarity on yet? Name it and close it, or accept it's a timing objection. A specific gap you can address is a next step. Vague hesitation is a signal to find the real objection.
→ Decision Criteria + Champion
