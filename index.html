How I Think Talent Development Revenue Enablement About

Learning is not
the program.
It's the foundation.

We often get caught up in learning events and completion rates rather than behavior change and talent culture. I look beyond the learning event to the foundational organizational design: competency frameworks, feedback loops, succession tools, in-platform delivery. I ask how learning can be embedded in the work rather than a separate event.

01
You can't develop people without a shared definition of performance. A competency framework is not an HR document. It's the foundation that makes feedback, succession, and development conversations possible.
02
The systems have to connect. A framework that lives in a PDF is not infrastructure. Feedback that doesn't trigger learning is a missed moment. Tools that require leaving the workflow don't get used.
03
The How matters as much as the What. Performance delivery (the What) is measured everywhere. The behaviors that make performance sustainable (the How): how people collaborate, develop others, instill trust, are less often captured.
