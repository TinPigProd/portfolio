Observation into structured development.

Most coaching conversations produce feedback that doesn't change behavior because it doesn't name a behavior. This system converts what managers actually observe into competency-anchored development goals a rep can act on.

01
Manager writes a raw observation Human
No template. No structure. Whatever the manager saw on the call, in whatever language comes naturally. Imprecise, subjective, and sometimes biased input is expected. The system is designed to handle it.
Example input"Good discovery but lost control when prospect asked about pricing. Seemed flustered. Also didn't confirm next steps at the end."
02
Agent maps to the GTM competency framework AI
The agent identifies the relevant competency domain, the behavioral indicator at the rep's current level, and the gap between what was observed and what the standard requires. The framework is embedded in the agent from the start, so output is anchored to it rather than labeled after the fact.
03
Manager reviews before delivery Human
The output is a draft, not a verdict. The manager reviews for accuracy, tone, and fairness. This is where judgment lives. The agent handles the structuring work so the manager can focus on whether the assessment is right, not whether it's formatted correctly.
04
Rep receives a specific, actionable development goal Human
Not "work on discovery." A named competency at a named level, with a specific behavioral indicator and a concrete next step to practice before the next call.
Example outputDiscovery and Qualification, Level 2. Observed: Initial pain surfaced but business impact not probed and cost of problem not quantified before moving to product. Development goal: Practice the MEDDIC impact and cost questions on the next three calls. Debrief with manager after each one.
