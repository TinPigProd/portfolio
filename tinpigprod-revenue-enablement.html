TruffleHQ · Revenue enablement

The field executes what enablement makes repeatable.

TruffleHQ is a fictional enterprise software company scaling a complex workforce intelligence platform, without a dedicated enablement function. This case study demonstrates how to build 0-to-1 enablement infrastructure from scratch, showcasing both the final production artifacts and the strategic systems behind them.

The opportunity for GTM enablement
01
Content gets built. Behavior doesn't change. Playbooks get written and filed. Battlecards get created and ignored. Reps build their own messaging from scratch anyway, because the enablement function produced artifacts instead of building the infrastructure that makes those artifacts actually land.
02
Ramp time is measured in weeks, not readiness. Day 30 means a calendar date, not a competency milestone. Reps get certified on product knowledge and turned loose on customers before they've demonstrated they can run a discovery conversation independently. Time becomes a proxy for readiness because readiness was never defined.
03
Enablement and revenue leadership operate in separate languages. Enablement reports on completion rates. Revenue leadership asks about pipeline. The two conversations never intersect because nobody built the measurement framework that translates one into the other. Enablement that can't prove its impact on business outcomes doesn't get to keep building.
