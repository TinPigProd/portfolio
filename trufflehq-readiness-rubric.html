TruffleHQ · CSM Readiness Rubric

Readiness is a milestone, not a calendar date.

A 0-to-90 day rubric for TruffleHQ Customer Success Managers. Each milestone defines what demonstrated competency looks like, how it gets observed, and what it gates. Time is a minimum threshold, not a measure of readiness.

Role: Customer Success Manager 3 milestone gates 5 competency domains
What Day 30 means: The CSM can navigate TruffleHQ's platform confidently, articulate the core value proposition without coaching, and has completed their first customer touchpoint with a manager present. They understand the TruffleHQ customer journey and can speak to their assigned accounts' goals and health status.

What Day 90 means: The CSM fully owns their book of accounts and is accountable for their renewal rate and expansion pipeline. They operate without manager oversight on routine customer interactions and are ready to be measured against the same metrics as tenured CSMs. Ramp ends here. Standard performance management begins.
