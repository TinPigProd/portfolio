TruffleHQ · Talk Tracks

The conversation, before the conversation.

Three talk tracks for three critical moments in the TruffleHQ sales motion. Not scripts to memorize. Mental models for what the conversation needs to accomplish and how to get there.

Goal: surface the real pain, connect it to a business cost, and earn the right to a demo. You should leave this call knowing exactly which persona problem is most acute and what a successful outcome looks like for them in 90 days.
01
Open with context, not a pitch
0–3 min
Rep
Thanks for making time. Before I get into anything about TruffleHQ, I want to make sure this conversation is actually useful to you. Can you tell me a bit about what prompted you to take this meeting? What's happening in your organization that made this feel worth 30 minutes?
Prospect
[Let them talk. Don't interrupt. What they lead with is the real problem.]
Listen for: The language they use to describe the problem. Are they talking about data gaps, talent visibility, AI readiness, or something else entirely? Mirror their language back, don't translate it into TruffleHQ vocabulary yet.
02
Deepen the pain
3–12 min
Rep
When you say you don't have visibility into your team's skills, help me understand what that means in practice. What decisions are you making right now without the data you wish you had?
Rep
What's the most expensive consequence of that gap? Has there been a specific situation where not having that picture cost you something concrete, time, money, a hire you shouldn't have made externally?
Rep
You mentioned the board is asking about AI readiness. How are you currently answering that question, and how confident do you feel in that answer?
Watch for: Specificity. Vague answers mean the pain isn't acute enough or you haven't found the right person yet. Specific dollar amounts, specific failed hires, specific board conversations mean you're in the right place.
03
MEDDIC qualification
12–20 min
Rep · M
If this was fully solved in 12 months, how would you measure that? What number would move, and what would that mean for your organization?
Rep · E
When you've bought platforms like this before, who was involved in the final decision? Is that the same group today, or has anything changed about how investments like this get approved?
Rep · D
When you evaluate options, what are the two or three things that matter most to you? Not what the vendor says matters, what actually matters to you and your team.
Rep · D
Walk me through how a decision like this would actually move forward. Who gets involved, in what order, and what does final approval look like on your end?
Rep · C
If I sent you a summary of what we've discussed today, would you be comfortable sharing it with your leadership team? Who else in your organization would benefit from seeing this picture?
MEDDIC reality check: If you can't answer all six elements after this call, the deal isn't qualified. Metrics, Economic Buyer, Decision Criteria, Decision Process, Identified Pain, and Champion. Missing any one of these is a gap to close before the demo, not after.
04
Connect to outcome, earn the demo
20–30 min
Rep
Based on what you've shared, it sounds like the core problem is [reflect their language back]. If you had a real-time picture of where those gaps actually are, what would you do differently in the next quarter?
Rep
I want to show you something specific in our platform that's directly relevant to what you described. But I want to make sure the demo is worth your time. Who else would need to be in that conversation for it to move forward?
If they can't name a next step or a decision-maker, the deal isn't real yet. Find out what has to be true for this to move.
Goal: show only what's relevant to the specific pain surfaced in discovery. Never run the full product tour. Every feature you show that doesn't connect to their stated problem is a reason for them to disengage.
01
Anchor to discovery
0–3 min
Rep
When we spoke last week, you mentioned [specific pain from discovery]. I've set up this demo specifically around that. I'm going to show you three things, and I want you to stop me if anything doesn't match how your organization actually works.
Rule: If you can't connect what you're about to show to something they said in discovery, don't show it.
02
Show the insight, not the feature
3–20 min
Rep
Here's what your organization's skills picture looks like in TruffleHQ. Not what people say they know, but what the platform infers from actual work patterns, project history, and performance signals. This updates continuously, so what you're looking at right now is current.
Rep
This is the AI readiness view your board is asking about. Instead of a survey, this is derived from what your people are actually doing. You can filter by team, by role, by the specific AI skills your strategy requires.
Rep
Here's the gap analysis for the role you mentioned struggling to hire for. TruffleHQ shows you the three people internally who are within six months of being ready for that role, with the specific skill development that would close the gap.
Pause after each section: "Does this match how you think about this problem? What are you seeing here that's different from what you expected?"
03
Close to next step
20–30 min
Rep
Based on what you've seen today, where does this land for you? Does this solve the problem you described, or is there something we didn't get to that matters more?
Rep
What would need to be true for this to move forward in the next 30 days? And who else needs to see this before that can happen?
Never leave a demo without a specific next step with a specific date. "I'll follow up" is not a next step.
Goal: get to a clear yes or a clear no. A maybe that extends indefinitely is worse than a no. The closing conversation surfaces what's actually blocking the deal and either resolves it or disqualifies the opportunity.
01
Surface the real objection
0–5 min
Rep
We've been talking for a while now and I want to be direct with you. What's actually standing between where we are today and a decision? I'd rather know the real answer than spend another month on calls.
Rep
If the budget, the timeline, and the stakeholder alignment were all in place, is TruffleHQ the right solution for what you described? I want to know if we have a fit problem or a timing problem, because those require different conversations.
Listen for: Fit objections need to be addressed or disqualified. Timing objections need a specific date when the conditions will be different. Vague objections mean you haven't found the real one yet.
02
Handle the final objections
5–20 min
Prospect
"We just need more time to evaluate."
Rep
What specifically are you trying to evaluate that you don't have clarity on yet? I want to make sure we're giving you what you need, not just waiting for a calendar date to pass. What would make you confident?
Prospect
"We need to get legal and procurement involved."
Rep
Completely expected. Let's get them started now so that process runs in parallel rather than becoming the long pole. What do they need from us to begin their review? I'll have our team send the standard security and legal package today.
03
Close or disqualify
20–30 min
Rep
Here's where I am: I believe TruffleHQ solves the problem you described. I want to help you get to a decision, in either direction, so neither of us is spending time on something that isn't going to move. What would it take to get to a yes or no in the next two weeks?
A clear no is better than an extended maybe. Disqualify with integrity and leave the door open for a future conversation when the conditions change.
